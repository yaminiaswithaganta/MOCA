MoCA app: how to start
======================

EASIEST WAY: open this folder in Claude Code (or another AI coding assistant) and
say: "Read SETUP_FOR_AI.md and walk me through it." It sets everything up, connects
Supabase, creates your admin account and can put the app online, telling you the
few things only you need to click.

Or do it yourself:

ONE-TIME SETUP (about 20 minutes, needs internet)
-------------------------------------------------
1. Install Python 3.12 from https://www.python.org/downloads/macos/
   and Node.js from https://nodejs.org (the LTS version).

2. Create a free project at https://supabase.com. Supabase holds the accounts,
   the database and the recordings.

3. In this folder, copy ".env.example" to a new file called ".env" and fill it in.
   Each line says where in the Supabase dashboard to find the value.
   In Supabase, add http://127.0.0.1:8000/** under
   Authentication > URL Configuration > Redirect URLs.

4. Right-click "start.command" and choose "Open", then click "Open" again.
   A black Terminal window sets everything up. When it's done, the app opens
   in your browser by itself.


5. Create your administrator account. In Terminal, in this folder, run:
     .venv/bin/python -m webapp.make_admin you@example.com
   Open the link it prints and choose a password.


EVERY TIME AFTER THAT
---------------------
- Double-click "start.command". The app opens in the browser in a few seconds.
- Keep the black Terminal window open while you use the app.
- Use Google Chrome. Click "Allow" when it asks for the microphone and camera.


USING IT
--------
Administrators: under "People", add examiners with their email and a password you
choose. Give them both: they sign in straight away. You can deactivate anyone or
set a new password. Administrators can't see test results.

Examiners: under "Patients", press "Add patient", enter the patient's name, email
and date of birth, and send them the link. Design tests under "Test designs", then
review, correct and release results under "Overview".

Patients: open the link from your examiner, enter your name, email and date of
birth, and start your test. The same link brings you back later. Your result
appears once your examiner releases it.


WHERE YOUR DATA IS
------------------
In your Supabase project: accounts, patients, tests, scores, recordings and photos.
Tests in progress also keep files in the results/ folder on the computer running
the app. This is private patient information, so keep it safe.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "Supabase isn't set up yet": check the values in the .env file, then restart.
- "Python 3.12 is needed": install Python, then double-click start.command again.
- No sound or the mic doesn't work: open "Mic & camera" in the app and check
  that Chrome is allowed to use the microphone (Chrome menu > Settings >
  Privacy and security > Site settings > Microphone).
